# **A Mouse for Natural Causes**

## A CMF design assignment

**Natural Causes** is a startup focused on creating practical technological devices designed for those who favor a less high-tech appearance and appreciate the transient nature of life.

Mice, keyboards, chargers, lamps, speakers, and other small electronic objects have remarkably short and useful lives but are often made from materials that remain on Earth *for centuries*. 

**We are asking you to do a color treatment for a mouse prototype that will be used to convey the possible color palettes of our brand.**

You will present us a painted computer mouse and share it as a CMF (Color, Material, and Finish) appearance prototype used to convey the possible color palettes of our brand. We are contracting you to develop a physical color experiment, or in design-speak, create a “boundary object,” to help us think about what a future slowly biodegradable version of our mouse product might look and feel like once we have put it into production.

### Our Premise

Natural Causes begins with a different premise: *objects, like us, don't need to last forever.* We even designed our company to not live forever. After 80 years, we will discontinue our operations.

The brand embraces usefulness, aging, wear, repair, decomposition, and eventual disappearance. The company imagines its products using biodegradable and lower-impact plastics wherever possible. Materials are selected with an object's *useful lifespan* in mind.

Natural Causes isn't overly morbid about our short time on earth, but we recognize that it can be funny and useful to recognize the uncomfortableness of our temporariness as humans. Knowing that something won't be here forever can make us pay closer attention to the time we have with it. And we can be respectful about this and a bit cheeky or humorous at the same time.

Natural Causes products want to be good products without becoming archaeological artifacts. 

### Our Tagline

Natural Causes has a working tagline. "Useful for your lifetime." This might help you connect with your work.

## **Brand Character**

Natural Causes should feel:

Useful, warm, quiet, tactile, slightly strange, practical, human, imperfect, and temporary.

It should **not** feel futuristic, cybernetic, militarized, gamer-oriented, or obsessed with artificial intelligence, tech-boosterism, or naive about the challenges the world is facing.

The Natural Causes visual language should avoid the familiar technological vocabulary of glossy black plastic, RGB lighting, carbon-fiber textures, aggressive geometry, neon accents, and the ever-tiring tech dork Pro / Max / Ultra /  product claims with ridiculous AI claims.

Look toward companies such as [Rapha Cycling](https://www.rapha.cc/), [Patagonia](https://www.patagonia.com/), [Snow Peak](https://www.snowpeak.com/), [Muji](https://www.muji.com/), [HAY](https://www.hay.com/), and [Bellroy](https://www.bellroy.com/) for examples of brands that have found ways to make highly functional objects feel connected to life beyond the computer.

Natural Causes should develop its own version of that sensibility, perhaps a little bit more cheeky and with the lightest sense of humor about our short time on earth.

## **CMF Direction**

The CMF design work (Color, Material, and Finish) should imagine technological objects that are comfortable with the idea that they will age.

*The challenge for this CMF work is to create a sophisticated contemporary technological object whose color, material, and finish acknowledge that permanence and tech-obsession aren't necessarily virtues.*

As our CMF designer, you should look toward colors that evoke natural processes such as:
- sprouting
- blooming
- unfurling
- weathering
- fading
- growing
- drying
- oxidizing
- bleaching in the sun
- returning to the ground

Colors that might have names more like:

clay, bone, mushroom, lichen, moss, faded blue, dusty rose, bark, sand, sage, ochre, warm gray, unbleached paper, dried grass, stone, soil.

Also, we don't want eco-looking beige products. Yuck. We believe in, and have, style. Elegant style.

Natural Causes is not pretending we don't need electronics. That would be ridiculous. Electronics are a necessary and useful evil for us living in the 2020s, but we at Natural Causes also don't believe we have to be tech freaks about them, free from reflexive thoughts. We also don't need to forfeit all our ecological responsibilities just to send a few emails and text messages to plan a party.

## **A Note About The Mouse Prototype**

We have given you a simple black plastic mouse that you can use to explore the color palettes of our brand. Today, unfortunately, Natural Causes *will* live forever because your mouse pre-production prototype is made of ABS plastic. ABS plastic fragments persist in the environment for *up to 1,000 years* after its initial mechanical breakdown in about 20 years. The synthetic polymers in ABS resist natural microbial decomposition, so it will be on the planet for almost 15 lifetimes. 

So…. ABS is pretty gross, but it is useful for us right now as we can quickly exploring form, color, finish, masking, and coating. We just wanted to let you know that ABS is *not* the material proposed for the eventual Natural Causes product, but we are where we are. We'll deal with ABS for now.

This mouse you are painting is an CMF appearance prototype used to convey the possible color palettes of our brand. It is a physical experiment, or in design-speak, a "boundary object," to help us think about what a future slowly biodegradable version of the product might look and feel like once we have put it into production.

### **1. Prime Your Prototype**

Apply gray primer to the entire mouse and bring the primed prototype to our next class for presentation.

Your primer coat should provide clean, consistent coverage across the complete object, including:

* Top surface and buttons  
* Both sides, including convex and concave surfaces  
* Entire bottom surface  
* Any recessed areas and edges  
* The *entire* scroll wheel. If we scroll the prototype, there should be no black plastic or silicone rubber showing.

Remember, rotate the scroll wheel between light coats of primer to achieve complete primer coverage of the wheel.

Before and after priming, you must handle and transport the primed prototype carefully. Use the plastic bag to assist this process. Scratches on your prototype are not allowed. Also, do not place the mouse in the provided Ziploc bag until the primer has dried. Bring it to class clean and free of scratches, fingerprints, or other damage.

A goal for the end of this first stage is a completely primed mouse.

### **2. Select Your Primary Color**

Choose one color from the provided spray paints as the primary color for your Natural Causes mouse. Tell us what that will be and that you will spray the mouse in this color during class on Wednesday, September 2.

Prepare a digital color swatch that approximates your selected spray color and be prepared to present it alongside your primed mouse prototype in class.

Your color choices should support the Natural Causes identity rather than simply being your favorite color. Consider what the color communicates about an object intended to feel human, useful, temporary, and comfortable with aging.

### **3. Develop a Natural Causes Color Palette**

Using your primary mouse color as a starting point, create a small CMF (Color, Material, and Finish) palette for Natural Causes. 

These colors could eventually appear on:

* scroll wheels  
* mouse cables or cable sleeves  
* buttons  
* product markings  
* accessories  
* packaging  
* other Natural Causes products

You may draw from the spray colors provided in the lab or propose your own colors.

Think about the palette as a family. Consider relationships between warm and cool colors, neutrals and accents, saturation, contrast, and how the colors might age together.

DO NOT create a collection of beige "eco" products. Look for softer, natural, weathered, faded, mineral, vegetal, or otherwise unexpected colors that give the brand a recognizable identity.

### **4. Give Your Mouse a Product Name**

Give your mouse product a working product name that fits the Natural Causes identity.

**Avoid** conventional technology naming such as:

- Mouse Pro X2  
- UltraClick  
- AI Mouse Max

Instead, think about the language already embedded in Natural Causes Brand Direction and CMF Direction. Consider usefulness, passages of time, weathering, daily life moments, and things eventually disappearing.

The name can be beautiful, mundane, dry, slightly dark, or lightly humorous (but not jokey).

Your name should help us imagine what kind of company Natural Causes is and how the mouse will fit into the brand.
