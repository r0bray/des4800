## Overview

Laser cutters are powerful digital fabrication tools that use a focused beam of light to cut through or engrave the surface of a wide range of materials. You can laser cut and engrave acrylic, wood, cardboard, leather, and fabric.

In this project you will learn the fundamentals of operating the machine safely and preparing vector files in Adobe Illustrator for cutting.

## **Your Project Tasks**

### **1. Prepare Your Design File**

Create a file in Illustrator that includes one 50mmx50mm cut-pathed shape. 

| First Letter of Last Name | Shape |
|---|---|
|A, B, or C| Triangle|
|D, E, or F| Nonagon|
|G, H, or I| Dodecahedron|
|J, K, or L| Pentagon|
|M, N, or O| Circle|
|P, Q, or R| Hexagon|
|S, T, or U| Rectangle|
|V, W, or X| Octagon|
|Y or Z| Square|

Examples
- if your last name is Jimenez, you will cut a Pentagon.
- if your last name is Walters, you will cut an Octagon.

> **🚨 NOTE!**
> Refer to [Preparing-Illustrator-for-Laser-Cutting.pdf](https://static.robray.net/images/Preparing-Illustrator-for-Laser-Cutting.pdf) for annotated screenshots and helpful tips.

- Set your artboard to match the size of the laser cutter bed 32" wide x 18"  high.
- Your Illustrator units of measurement should be millimeters.
- Use a lefthand and bottom guide in your Illustrator file to align your design with the bottom-left of the laser cutter bed with a little bit of margin.
- Use the shape assigned to your name in the table above.
- The shape should fit within 50mm x 50mm dimensions.
- Your outline must be set as hairline stroke of 0.1 px in the designated RED rgb  color. R = 255, G = 0, B = 0. The ULS Laser Cutter will use this as the laser's cut path.
- Your file should be saved as _firstname-lastname-lasershape.ai_ file.

### **2. Prepare the Equipment**

Before running your job:
1. Power on the fume extractor.
2. Power on the laser cutter.
3. Load and secure your scrap piece of acrylic material on the cutting bed.

### **3. Run Your Job**

Did you prepare the equipment (extractor on, laser on, material loaded)? If not, do so now. If so, great!

1. Stay present and attentive while the machine is running. DO NOT EVER leave it unattended.
2. Print your file to the laser cutter. Once the file is received you will see a FILE option on the laser cutter's screen
3. Use the FILE menu on the laser cutter to set the correct speed and power settings for your material. For acrylic, this is typically 
Power = 100% Speed = 4% but always run a test job to confirm settings before running the actual job.
4. Typically you will always want to run a test job to confirm settings before running the actual job. This will help you verify that your material is loaded correctly and that the laser cutter settings are what you want. But for this project your test cut is a small, simple shape so you can quickly verify that your settings are correct.

### **4. Finish**

Once your piece is complete:

- Clean up any residue or char on the edges of your cut.
- Remove any masking tape or other material from the acrylic surfaces.
- Ensure you remove any remaininng leftover materials from the laser cutter.
- If nobody else is in line to use the laser cutter, power down the equipment.
- Save your file before closing Illustrator, close Illustrator and log out of the computer.
- Take a landscape orientation photo of your laser cut shape and include it in the [zip archive](http://localhost:4321/projects/introduction-to-laser-cutting/#upload-a-zip-archive-file-to-canvas-that-includes-2-files) you will upload to Canvas.
