## Overview

Laser cutters are powerful digital fabrication tools that use a focused beam of light to cut through or engrave the surface of a wide range of materials. You can laser cut and engrave acrylic, wood, cardboard, leather, and fabric.

In this project you will learn the fundamentals of operating the machine safely and preparing vector files in Adobe Illustrator for cutting.

---

## **Your Project Tasks**

### **1. Prepare Your Design File**

Create a file in Illustrator that includes at least one cut-pathed shape.

- Cut paths should be set as hairline strokes (0.1 pt) in the designated RED (rgb)color.
- Set your artboard to match the size of the laser cutter bed 32" wide x 18"  high.
- Your units of measurement should be millimeters.
- Use a lefthand and bottom guide to align your design with the bottom-left of the laser cutter bed with a little bit of margin.
- Your file should be saved as a .ai file.

### **2. Prepare the Equipment**

Before running your job:
1. Power on the fume extractor.
2. Power on the laser cutter.
3. Load and secure your scrap piece of acrylic material on the cutting bed.

### **3. Run Your Job**

Did you prepare the equipment (extractor on, laser on, material loaded)? If not, do so now. If so, great!

1. Stay present and attentive while the machine is running. DO NOT EVER leave it unattended.
2. Print your file to the laser cutter. Once the file is received you will see a FILE option on the laser cutter's screen
3. Use the FILE menu on the laser cutter to set the correct speed and power settings for your material. For acrylic, this is typically 
Power = 100% Speed = 4% but always run a test job to confirm settings before running the actual job.
5. Run a test job to confirm settings before running the actual job. This will help you verify that your material is loaded correctly and that the laser cutter settings are what you want. 

### **4. Finish**

Once your piece is complete:

- Clean up any residue or char on the edges of your cut.
- Remove any masking tape from the acrylic surfaces.
- Ensure you remove any remaininng leftover materials from the laser cutter.
- If nobody else is in line to use the laser cutter, power down the equipment.
- Save your file before closing Illustrator, close Illustrator and log out of the computer.
